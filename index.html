Uploaders: which team are you?

Pick once. Your team fills in automatically every time you tap Submit.

How to play

  1. Do the challenge as a team. Everyone plays.
  2. Your team's uploader taps Submit on that challenge. The form opens with the challenge already picked.
  3. Add your photo or video and hit submit. Then come back for the next one.

Videos under 15 seconds unless the challenge says otherwise. No signal? Shoot it now, upload later. Deadlines: Friday and Saturday at lights-out, Sunday before we head home. Nothing happens at the museums: those are just for exploring. Scores are revealed along the way. Final scores and the winner on Sunday. 🏆

Have fun. Be safe. Be kind.
